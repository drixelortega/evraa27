About the games

About EVRAA 2027

The Eastern Visayas Regional Athletic Association meet, March 31 – April 5, 2027, hosted by Calbayog City and Catbalogan City.

EVRAA 2027 is more than a sporting event. It is our commitment to excellence, our celebration of unity, and our opportunity to showcase the very best of Calbayog City and Catbalogan City.

The theme

One Region. One Spirit. One Victory.

01

One Region

Whatever province, city or school they represent, every delegate belongs to one regional community with a shared vision of excellence and development.

02

One Spirit

Sportsmanship, discipline, teamwork, respect and perseverance: the values that unite every athlete, coach, official and supporter beyond the competition.

03

One Victory

The real triumph goes beyond medals: building character, lifelong friendships and unity, and inspiring the youth toward excellence in sports and in life, for a stronger, healthier, more united Eastern Visayas.

The logo

What the EVRAA 2027 logo means

Every element honors the region and its two host cities, and together they carry the theme: all of Eastern Visayas coming together as one regional family, united by shared values and cultural pride.

  • The figure 8

    Stands for Region VIII. Its infinity-inspired form signifies the region's enduring unity, limitless potential and continuous pursuit of excellence, and it celebrates every province, city, municipality and school.

  • The EVRAA lettering

    Bold and forward-leaning for strength, momentum and progress. Its green stands for growth, harmony and resilience.

  • Feather & fish scales

    The golden feather honors Calbayog's Hadang Festival: grace, creativity and cultural pride. The blue fish scales honor Catbalogan's Manaragat Festival: the sea, fishing heritage, abundance and resilience.

  • The torch

    The universal symbol of sport. Its rising yellow-to-red flame carries the region's hope and aspirations, and the energy, passion and courage of everyone in the games.
